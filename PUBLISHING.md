## Summary

Choose where to mute minimap ping sounds while keeping ping markers visible.

## Description

Minimap Pinger lets you silence the minimap ping sound separately in the open world, dungeons, raids, scenarios and Delves, battlegrounds, and arenas. Open the settings with `/mmp` or through Options → AddOns → Minimap Ping Sound, then check the places where you want pings muted. All options are off by default, and your choices are saved.

The addon mutes the game's ping sound file while a selected option applies. Other alerts that reuse the same sound file may also be silenced in that content. Ping markers remain visible.
